## Rules applied

- **No billing:** never enable billing on Google Cloud or Groq projects used by Life OS.
- **Fallbacks:** every AI call has a deterministic fallback and a hard per-run cap. Topic extraction handles at most 6 documents per request.
- **No new hosting:** no service that needs its own server is adopted.
